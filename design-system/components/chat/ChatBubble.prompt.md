Message bubble for the AI Chatbot panel — bot messages sit on a white card, user messages on translucent white over the dark chat surface, both right-angled on the "tail" corner.

```jsx
<ChatBubble from="user">How many calories did I burn yesterday?</ChatBubble>
<ChatBubble from="bot" timestamp="2 hours ago">You burned 420 kcal across 3 activities</ChatBubble>
```
